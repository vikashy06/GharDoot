# GharDoot – Official Brand Identity & Design Guidelines

**Brand Name:** GharDoot  
**Brand Descriptor:** Your Local Delivery Partner  
**Official Tagline:** Har Zaroorat, Ghar Tak!  
**Business Concept:** A village-based hyperlocal grocery and daily essentials delivery platform connecting customers with existing local shops.

---

## 1. Brand Concept & Etymology

- **Ghar (घर):** The home, household, family hearth, and neighborhood sanctuary in rural India. Represents warmth, safety, and trusted doorstep service.
- **Doot (दूत):** Messenger, envoy, swift courier, and carrier of goodwill and essentials.
- **Combined Meaning:** *The Village Home Messenger* – delivering groceries and essentials from local neighborhood kirana stores directly to residents' homes in 20–30 minutes.

---

## 2. Brand Identity Requirements & Rules

1. **Official Brand Name:**
   - Always spell as **GharDoot** (Capital "G", lowercase "har", capital "D", lowercase "oot").
   - Displayed as the official brand name throughout the website, mobile applications, merchant terminals, and admin panels.
   - **Strict prohibition:** Do NOT use the previous legacy brand name anywhere in any code, UI, or document.

2. **Brand Descriptor:**
   - **"Your Local Delivery Partner"**
   - Appears directly below the brand name on headers, store banners, delivery bags, rider uniforms, and marketing assets.

3. **Official Tagline:**
   - **"Har Zaroorat, Ghar Tak!"**
   - Must appear prominently on the homepage hero section and the platform footer.

4. **Professional Green & White Color Palette:**
   - **Forest Green (Primary):** `#15803d` / RGB(21, 128, 61) – Represents agriculture, rural prosperity, freshness, and dependability.
   - **Emerald Action (Secondary):** `#16a34a` / RGB(22, 163, 74) – Used for primary interactive actions and buttons.
   - **Leaf Green (Accent):** `#22c55e` / RGB(34, 197, 94) – Used for active status badges, notifications, and speed accents.
   - **Mint Light:** `#86efac` / RGB(134, 239, 172) – Used for subtle glows and highlights.
   - **Village Meadow (Surface Tint):** `#f0fdf4` – Gentle light green background tint.
   - **Pure White:** `#ffffff` – Clean white cards, surface containers, and inverted logo elements.
   - **Charcoal Forest (Text):** `#0f172a` / `#14532d` – High contrast typography.

5. **Logo Concept (Home + Delivery):**
   - Seamlessly blends the silhouette of a rural Indian cottage/home roofline with a speed-winged parcel delivery box and delivery wheels/motion trails.
   - Available formats:
     - `assets/images/logo.svg`: Full horizontal master logo (on light backgrounds)
     - `assets/images/logo-white.svg`: Inverted logo (on dark green/footer backgrounds)
     - `assets/images/logo-icon.svg`: App Icon & Favicon badge
     - `assets/images/favicon.svg`: Browser tab favicon

6. **Consistent Cross-Dashboard Branding:**
   - Shared design language across all 4 stakeholder portals:
     - **Customer Storefront & Orders:** `index.html` & `customer-dashboard.html`
     - **Local Shopkeeper Hub:** `shopkeeper-dashboard.html`
     - **Delivery Partner (Doot) Hub:** `delivery-dashboard.html`
     - **Platform Admin Control:** `admin-dashboard.html`
     - **Interactive Brand Book:** `brand-identity.html`
