Business Concept & Mission

GharDoot is a village-based hyperlocal grocery and daily essentials delivery platform connecting customers with existing local shops.

Unlike urban dark-store delivery models that displace neighborhood stores, GharDoot champions and empowers local village Kiranas, dairies, and sabzi mandis. By mobilizing local rural youth as "GharDoots" (delivery messengers/heroes), we provide village residents with 20–30 minute doorstep delivery.

Etymology of GharDoot:
• Ghar (घर): Home, family, hearth, warmth, and sanctuary in rural India.
• Doot (दूत): Messenger, trusted envoy, swift courier, and carrier of goodwill.

Positioned directly below the brand name GharDoot on header lockups, delivery partner uniforms, merchant storefront signage, and app store listings.

Compliance Checklist

Brand Integrity Guidelines

✓ MANDATORY DO'S

  • • Display GharDoot as the official brand name across the entire application and all stakeholder views.
  • • Use "Your Local Delivery Partner" below the brand name in headers, footers, and official badges.
  • • Display "Har Zaroorat, Ghar Tak!" on the homepage hero section and footer.
  • • Maintain consistent green and white branding across Customer, Shopkeeper, Delivery Partner, and Admin dashboards.
  • • Preserve the custom Home + Delivery logo mark with its correct proportions and clear space.

✕ STRICT DON'TS

  • • DO NOT use the previous legacy brand name anywhere in the final application, codebase, documentation, or user interfaces.
  • • Never alter or skew the home + delivery parcel proportions in the logo.
  • • Never use unauthorized color palettes (e.g. orange, purple or blue) for the primary brandmark.
  • • Do not present GharDoot as an urban dark-store platform — it is strictly village-based and hyperlocal connecting existing shops.
