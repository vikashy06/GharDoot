The Rural Challenge

Why GharDoot Was Born

In millions of villages across India, elderly grandparents, busy farm families, and mothers managing households often face hardship travelling 2 to 5 kilometers on unpaved roads to fetch urgent daily essentials like milk, cooking oil, baby soap, or salt.

Meanwhile, urban quick-commerce companies burn millions building artificial "dark stores" that destroy small mom-and-pop Kirana stores. GharDoot chose a different path:

The GharDoot Solution:
• Connect existing, beloved village Kirana merchants directly with neighborhood homes.
• Train local village youth with bicycles and motorbikes as dignified "GharDoots" (delivery partners).
• Deliver orders in 15 to 30 minutes at authentic counter rates with Cash on Delivery (COD) and scan-and-pay UPI.
